# KPI definitions (plain English)

Human-written definitions of the headline KPIs. `metric_definitions.md` is generated by
`scripts/build_metric_catalogue.py` and is overwritten on every rebuild, so these live here instead.
Several KPIs below are *derived* (calculated from two or more catalogue metrics) and so have no
row of their own in the generated table.

| KPI | Formula (catalogue metric_ids) | Unit | Better | Plain-English definition | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GHG intensity per Rs crore | (`ghg_scope1_tco2e` + `ghg_scope2_tco2e`) / (`turnover_inr` / 1e7) | tCO2e per Rs crore | Lower | How many tonnes of greenhouse gas the company emits from its own operations and the electricity it buys, for every Rs 1 crore of revenue it earns. It lets a large and a small company be compared fairly, because it measures how carbon-heavy each rupee of business is, not how big the company is. | We calculate it ourselves from corrected emissions and corrected turnover. The intensity companies file is used only as a cross-check: just 33 of 50 FY26 filed values matched our calculation within 5%. Compare companies within the same sector; a bank and a steel maker are not comparable. |
| Renewable energy share % | `energy_renewable_gj` / `energy_total_gj` x 100 | % | Higher | Out of all the energy the company used in the year, the percentage that came from renewable sources such as solar, wind or hydro. It shows how far the company has moved away from fossil fuels. | FY23 filings call total energy `TotalEnergyConsumption` (taxonomy rename, mapped in the catalogue). A high share at a very small energy user (e.g. an office-only company) means less than the same share at a factory. |
| Waste recovery rate % | `waste_recovered_t` / `waste_generated_t` x 100 | % | Higher | Out of all the waste the company produced, the percentage it recycled, re-used or otherwise recovered instead of burning or landfilling it. It measures how circular the company's operations are. | Mining overburden (e.g. Coal India, ~5.3 billion t) dwarfs everyone else, so only compare within sector and use the rate, never total tonnes. Check that recovered + disposed is close to generated; if not, the inputs are inconsistent. |
| Female wage share % | `female_wage_share_pct` (as filed) | % | Higher | The share of the company's total gross wages that is paid to women. Read it next to the female share of the workforce: if women are 30% of staff but get 16% of wages, the 14-point gap suggests women are concentrated in lower-paid roles. | Filed from FY2023-24 only (BRSR Core). Filed as a decimal (0.2036 = 20.36%). On its own it mixes two things, how many women are employed and how they are paid, so always pair it with female workforce share (from `headcount`). |
| LTIFR | `ltifr` (as filed), shown separately for Employees and Workers | injuries per 1 million hours worked | Lower | Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate: how many work injuries serious enough to make someone miss work happened for every one million hours worked. Using hours, not headcount, makes companies of different sizes comparable. | Only reported split into Employees and Workers; workers (shop floor, sites) usually have much higher rates, so never average the two. A rate of 0 can mean genuinely safe or weak recording; read it with recordable injuries and fatalities. |

## Gold KPIs (`fct_company_year`, Week 7)

Computed by us from the latest current-year values and the corrected turnover
(`turnover_cr` = turnover in INR / 1e7). Any division by zero or by a missing value gives empty;
intensities are empty when turnover is missing. Percent columns are on a 0-100 scale.

| KPI | Formula | Better | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ghg_intensity_tco2e_per_cr` | (Scope 1 + Scope 2) / `turnover_cr` | Lower | The filed intensity is kept as `ghg_intensity_filed_per_cr`; `ghg_intensity_filed_vs_computed_ratio` = filed / computed, expected near 1. In the current data only 89 of 196 company-years are within 0.9-1.1, because filed intensities use different denominators and units. |
| `renewable_share_pct` | renewable energy / total energy x 100 | Higher | |
| `energy_intensity_gj_per_cr` | total energy / `turnover_cr` | Lower | |
| `water_intensity_kl_per_cr` | water consumption / `turnover_cr` | Lower | Consumption, not withdrawal. |
| `waste_recovery_rate_pct` | waste recovered / waste generated x 100 | Higher | Above 100 means recovered includes past stock; flagged by the warn test. |
| `waste_intensity_t_per_cr` | waste generated / `turnover_cr` | Lower | |
| `waste_balance_gap_pct` | (generated - recovered - disposed) / generated x 100 | Near 0 | A consistency check: far from 0 means the three waste figures do not add up. |
| `fatalities_per_10k_workforce` | fatalities (employees + workers) / `headcount_total` x 10,000 | Lower | `any_fatality` = fatalities > 0. |
| `female_workforce_pct` | female employees + workers / `headcount_total` x 100 | Higher | **Headcount basis:** employees + workers, permanent and other than permanent (the "Employees" and "Workers" totals). |
| `pay_equity_gap_pp` | `female_wage_share_pct` - `female_workforce_pct` | Higher (closer to 0) | Percentage points. Negative = women receive a smaller share of wages than their share of headcount. Empty before FY2023-24 (wage share is a BRSR Core metric). |
| `attrition_gap_pp` | female - male attrition rate, permanent employees | Lower | Percentage points. |
| `recordable_injuries_total`, `fatalities_total` | employees + workers | Lower | Empty only if both are missing. |
| `*_annualised` | quantity x 12 / `period_months` | | Only filled for years that are not 12 months (NESTLEIND FY2023-24, 15 months). |

### Peer percentile (`fct_company_kpi_percentile`)

Each company is placed within its **ranking group** (peer group of at least 5 companies, else
sector group) for each year and KPI, with 100 = best:

- percent rank = (rank - 1) / (n - 1), ties share the lower rank, among companies with a value;
- lower-is-better KPIs are ranked from the highest value down, so the lowest value scores 100;
- only given when the group has at least 5 companies with a value, the KPI is material for the
  group (`metric_materiality`), and the KPI has a direction; otherwise empty with
  `percentile_null_reason` (`group_n_lt_5`, `not_material`, `no_direction`, `no_value`).

`fct_peer_benchmark` holds the group distribution (n, median, 25th / 75th percentile, min, max) for
the same KPIs. Levels include years with a comparability break; only year-on-year comparisons
should exclude them.
