## Live evaluation

How each question is scored: **right tool** - every expected tool was called (for a question that must be refused: no tool at all); **numbers right** - every expected number is in the answer within its tolerance (questions that have numbers); **refusals right** - the three out-of-scope questions are refused; **wrongly refused** - answerable questions the model refused; **caveat check** - no caveat that does not apply (the Disclosure Quality Score caveat in an answer about another figure, "provisional" for a final year); **mentions** - a withheld figure is called withheld, the provisional year is called provisional, expected words appear, no causal wording, and the caveat check. A question passes when right tool, numbers and mentions all pass.

**Not scored** - the provider could not answer at all (a server error such as HTTP 504, or a timeout) even after one retry of the request and one retry of the question. That is an outage, not a model failure, so it is not counted against the model: the pass rate is given over all questions and over the answered ones, and every other column is over the answered questions only. Re-run such questions with `--only`: a re-run replaces a row only if that question was not scored. A question that was already scored keeps its first scored answer as the official result, and the re-run is reported as **run-to-run variability** (last column), not counted. Runs saved before the caveat check existed are re-checked on their saved answers.

(`q03`, `q12`, `q15`) and one is a wrong-tool fix (`q11`). In v1, `q03` asked for `Asset-heavy` as a

ranking_group)", and the model corrected itself to `peer_group` and gave 68.1%, provisional.

to (v1 used `restatement_summary` and gave the all-years share). This is one run per version on a
free-tier model: v1 `q12` also passed on its own re-run (variability, above), so a difference of
this size is encouraging, not proof.

**The one remaining failure, `q10`, is new in v2 (a regression).** Question: the share of all
checkable figures restated materially (expected: 6.7%, the all-years figure from
`restatement_summary(level=overall)`). v1 got it right. In v2 the model called
`group_metric(material_restatement_share, All companies, FY2023-24)`, picked a year the question

is invented, but it is not the one asked for. Likely cause (an inference from the call, not
something the log proves): the v2 description of `restatement_summary` says it is "not for the share
in one specific year" and that group_metric holds the per-year share, which pushed a model that has
to choose a year towards `group_metric`. The description was written to fix `q11` and over-corrects
for a question that names no year. Per the rules of this evaluation it was not changed after seeing
the result; a fix would be a separate, labelled version.

**Held-out: 5 of 5**, with no group-type error and no refused call: `Large` (`size_band`), `Cement &
construction` (withheld, `peer_group`) and `Healthcare` (provisional, `peer_group`) were sent with the
